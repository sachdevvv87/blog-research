**Prepared:** 18 November 2026

**Method:** Merchant X-Ray Applied To A 12-Post Sample, Plus Title-Level Review Of The Wider Archive

## 1. Read This First: Limits Of This Analysis

- **Reference Files Missing.** Only `SKILL.md` Was Available. The Official Persona Definitions, Scoring Weights, Verdict Thresholds, And QeRetail Context File Were Not. Nothing Below Comes From Those Files.
- **Scoring Assumptions.** Overall Score Is The Equal-Weight Mean Of The Seven Dimensions (The Skill's Stated Fallback). Verdict Bands Are Assumed: Pass 8.0 And Above, Pass With Changes 6.5 To 7.9, Revise 4.5 To 6.4, Reject Below 4.5. A Credibility Problem Can Override The Band.
- **Personas Are Placeholders.** Anthony, Jacob, Maria, And Charlie Are Simulated Perspectives Without Official Definitions. Assumed Lenses: Anthony (Revenue And Payback), Jacob (Operations And Technical Risk), Maria (Brand And Customer Experience), Charlie (Budget Skeptic). Treat Their Reactions As Hypotheses, Not Customer Research.
- **Sample, Not Census.** The Archive Holds Roughly 100 Posts Or More, And Every Index Page Has A Load More Button That Could Not Be Expanded. 12 Posts Received A Full Review. The Rest Were Assessed By Title, Date, And Category Only. Gaps Marked "Inference" Need Confirmation Against The Full Archive.
- **No Search Data.** No Keyword Or Traffic Tool Was Connected, So Search Demand Is Not Measured.
- **Format.** Delivered As Markdown. Calibri And Black Text Must Be Applied When Converting To Docx Or Slides.
- **Source Rule.** Statistics Are Judged Against The House Rule (Gartner, Deloitte, McKinsey, Accenture, IBM, Published 2025 Or Later). No New Statistics Were Introduced Here.

## 2. Executive Summary

1. **The Recent Shopify Cluster Is Strong. The Re-Dated Legacy Layer Is Hurting It.** Posts From August To November 2026 Score 6.1 To 8.1. Four Legacy Posts Re-Stamped With 2025 Or 2026 Dates Score 3.7 To 4.5.
2. **No Post In The Sample Contains A Named Merchant Result.** 0 Of 12. The Site Has A Case Studies Section That The Blog Never Draws On. This Is The Largest Credibility Gap.
3. **No Post Contains Original Data.** Every Statistic Is Third-Party. Only 3 Of 12 Have A Worked Example With Numbers.
4. **Every Post Ends In The Same Generic Offer.** "Book A Free Audit" Or "Let's Talk" Appears 4 To 5 Times Per Post Regardless Of Reader Stage.
5. **Topic Coverage Is Narrow.** Black Friday And Cyber Monday, Plan Economics, And Store Tidy-Up Topics Dominate. Several Revenue-Relevant Subjects For $100K To $500K Stores Are Missing (Section 6).
6. **Recent Sourcing Is Mostly Good.** Newer Posts Cite McKinsey, Deloitte, Accenture, Gartner, And IBM. Weak Spots Are Missing Publication Years And A Few Non-Approved Sources.

### 6.3 Funnel And Offer Gaps

- **Heavy Middle And Bottom, Thin Top.** Almost Every Recent Post Targets A Store Already Weighing Options. Few Posts Serve An Owner Who Does Not Yet Know The Problem Exists.
- **No Stage-Matched CTAs.** One Offer ("Book A Free Audit," "Talk To A Strategist," "Let's Talk") Repeats 4 To 5 Times Per Post. A Reader Of A Calculator Post And A Reader Of A Settings Post Get The Same Ask.
- **No Mid-Funnel Asset.** Calculators, Templates, Or Scorecards Would Let Readers Take A Smaller Step Than A Consultation.
- **Contradiction.** Post 7 Advises Limiting Pop-Ups While The Page Itself Shows A Newsletter Pop-Up.
- **Sales Voice In Informational Posts.** Post 4 Includes A "How QeRetail Helps" Section Of Six Services Inside A Fix-First Guide. That Dilutes Trust.

### 6.4 Audience Fit Gaps

- **Stated Audience.** $100K To $500K Shopify Stores.
- **Legacy Layer.** Written For Beginners, "New-In-Business" Sellers, And Platform Shoppers Of 2018 To 2021. Mismatch With The Stated Buyer.
- **Revenue Tiers Lack A Time Basis.** Posts 7, 8, 9 Use Tiers Without Saying Annual Or Monthly, And Without Currency (One Post Has A Descending Range, 400K-200K).
- **Developer Content Aimed At Owners.** Post 5 Is Useful To A Developer But Not Framed For The Person Who Signs The Contract.

### 6.5 Freshness And Quality Control Gaps

- **Re-Dated Legacy Posts.** At Least Four Posts With Date Stamps Of 2025 Or 2026 Carry Earlier Years In Their Title Or Body: Pet Products (Title 2024), Homepage Elements (Title 2024), Instagram Marketplace (Title October 2023), 25-Point Checklist (Title Holiday 2023). The Instagram Post Carries January 2026 As Its Date.
- **Index Ordering.** Archive Pages Mix Dates Out Of Order, Which Confuses Readers And Search Engines.
- **Editing Errors.** Truncated Sentences In Post 10, Duplicate Phrase ("Contact Us With Us") In Post 11, Plan Name Drift (Grow Versus Growth) In Post 1.
- **Site Defects Seen On Several Pages.** Navigation Links Missing A Slash (Example: `qeretail.comfree-estore-design`), A `javascript:void()` Typo, And "Coming Soon" Placeholder Links.
- **Internal Links.** Contextual Links Inside Posts Point To A Few Guides (Checkout Optimization, CAC Reduction, Funnel Guides) That Do Not Appear In The Index. The "Also Read" Block Repeats The Same Three Posts Across The Cluster. Verify The Targets Exist.
- **Author Consistency.** Legacy Bylines (Alice Poppins, Andy Thomas, Stephen Meyer) Show No Bio Or Title.

## 7. What Works (Keep And Scale)

- **Worked Margin Math (Post 3).** The Clearest Example Of Content A Merchant Can Act On. Numbers Reconcile Within One Cent.
- **Revenue-Tier Advice.** Giving Different Next Steps For Different Store Sizes Is Specific And Differentiating.
- **Opening Lines With A Number Or A Specific Problem.** Posts 1, 2, 3, 5 Start With Concrete Claims.
- **Key Action Points, Quick Wins, FAQ Structure.** Scannable And Answer-Engine Friendly.
- **Approved-Source Discipline In Recent Posts.** McKinsey, Deloitte, Accenture, Gartner, And IBM Appear Repeatedly.
- **Honest Boundaries.** "When Is An Upgrade The Wrong Move?" In Post 1 Builds Trust By Not Selling.
