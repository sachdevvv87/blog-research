## What Are Shopify Plus, Adobe Commerce, and Custom Commerce?

Shopify Plus, Adobe Commerce, and custom commerce are the three main routes to an enterprise ecommerce platform: a fully hosted SaaS plan, a deeply extensible platform built on Magento, and a storefront and back end engineered to your own specification.

Here is what each option means in practice:

- **Shopify Plus:** Shopify's enterprise plan, [starting at $2,300 USD per month](https://www.shopify.com/plus/pricing). Shopify runs hosting, security, PCI compliance, and core updates. You extend it with apps, Shopify Functions, Checkout UI extensions, and APIs.
- **Adobe Commerce:** The licensed enterprise edition of Magento. It runs on Adobe Commerce Cloud (PaaS), on your own servers, or, since June 2025, as [Adobe Commerce as a Cloud Service](https://business.adobe.com/blog/faster-growth-with-the-new-adobe-commerce-optimizer) (SaaS). You get full code access and a native B2B module.
- **Custom commerce:** A platform you build and own, either from scratch or from composable services such as commercetools, an open-source engine like Medusa, a headless CMS, and your own front end. There's no license fee, but every upgrade is yours to plan and pay for.

All three have analyst backing. According to the vendors, Shopify (third straight year), Adobe (ninth), and commercetools (sixth) were each named Leaders in the [2025 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Digital Commerce](https://www.gartner.com/en/documents/7128430), which assessed 19 vendors.

The real difference isn't features. It's who owns the upgrade path: Shopify, you and Adobe together, or only you.

## Ecommerce Platform Comparison: How Do the Three Options Differ?

Shopify Plus leads on speed to launch and low operating overhead. Adobe Commerce leads on deep customization and native complex B2B. Custom commerce wins only when your commerce logic is itself your competitive advantage.

Here is how the three compare across the factors that drive an enterprise platform decision:

| Factor | Shopify Plus | Adobe Commerce | Custom Commerce |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hosting model | SaaS, fully managed by Shopify | PaaS (Commerce Cloud), on-premise, or SaaS (Cloud Service) | Your cloud account (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) |
| Entry cost | From $2,300/month plus a GMV-based fee at scale | GMV-tiered annual license, estimated $22K–$190K+/year | No license; $25K–$150K (mid-market) to $1M+ (enterprise) to build |
| Upgrades | Automatic; no version debt | Quarterly patches; each release line has a fixed end-of-support date | You plan, fund, and test every upgrade |
| Customization depth | Apps, Functions, Checkout UI extensions; checkout logic runs in a sandbox | Full source-code access to any module | Unlimited |
| Native B2B | Company profiles, unlimited catalogs, payment terms, deposits | Company accounts, shared catalogs, quotes, requisition lists, approvals | Whatever you build |
| Multi-store | 9 expansion stores included | Multiple sites and store views from one instance | Whatever you build |
| Team you need | Shopify developers plus app governance | Specialist Adobe Commerce developers and DevOps | A full product engineering team |
| Best for | DTC and mid-market B2B brands that value speed | Large catalogs, complex B2B, heavy customization | Business models no platform supports |

So, the comparison table doesn't pick a winner. Your requirements list does.

Features narrow the shortlist. Cost usually decides it.

## How Much Do Shopify Plus, Adobe Commerce, and Custom Commerce Cost?

Shopify Plus starts at $2,300 per month. Adobe Commerce licenses are tiered by GMV and typically estimated at $22,000 to $190,000+ per year. Custom commerce has no license fee, but a build usually costs $25,000–$150,000 for a mid-market store and $400,000 to $1M+ for enterprise programs.

Each option is priced on a different unit: a monthly subscription plus a share of revenue, a GMV-tiered annual license, or engineering hours. So comparing sticker prices rarely works. Compare three-year total cost instead.

### Shopify Plus Pricing

Shopify Plus pricing has two parts: a fixed monthly subscription and, at higher volumes, a variable platform fee. As of November 2026, Shopify's pricing page lists Plus as ["starts at $2,300 USD/mo"](https://www.shopify.com/plus/pricing). That rate applies to a 3-year term; 1-year terms are [widely reported at about $2,500/month](https://elogic.co/blog/shopify-plus-pricing-2026/).

Higher-volume merchants also pay a variable platform fee that Shopify quotes privately. Agencies report it starting at about $800,000 in monthly GMV, at roughly 0.35% (3-year) or 0.40% (1-year) for D2C and 0.18% for B2B, with a cap near $40,000/month. Shopify doesn't publish these figures, so get your rate in writing.

The Plus plan also includes:

- **Payments:** Shopify Payments card rates from 2.25% + 30¢ (US).
- **Expansion stores:** [Nine expansion stores](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/intro-to-shopify/pricing-plans/plans-features/shopify-plus-plan) for regions, brands, or wholesale.
- **Retail:** Up to 200 POS Pro locations.
- **B2B:** Unlimited B2B catalogs.

### Adobe Commerce Pricing

Adobe doesn't publish Adobe Commerce pricing. Licenses are quoted against the GMV and AOV tiers in [Adobe's own tier table](https://www.adobe.com/content/dam/cc/en/legal/terms/enterprise/pdfs/AdobeCommerceGMV-AOVTable.pdf), which runs from Tier 1 (up to $50 million GMV) to Tier 7 (up to $900 million).

Agency estimates for 2026 [put licenses at](https://ecommerce.folio3.com/blog/how-much-does-adobe-commerce-cost/):

- **On-premise:** about $22,000/year under $1M GMV, rising to about $125,000/year above $25M.
- **Adobe Commerce Cloud:** about $40,000/year under $1M GMV, rising to $190,000+/year above $25M.

The license is only 20%–40% of annual spend. A mid-market B2B Adobe Commerce store runs roughly [$122,000–$450,000 a year all-in](https://elogic.co/blog/replatforming-cost-index/). The license isn't the expensive part. The team that keeps the platform current is.

### Custom Ecommerce Development Cost

Custom ecommerce development has no license fee, but a custom mid-market store costs about [$25,000 to $150,000, and complex enterprise and composable programs about $400,000 to $1 million or more](https://elogic.co/blog/ecommerce-development-cost/). Enterprise commerce ecosystems take [9–18 months to deliver](https://zoolatech.com/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-build-an-ecommerce-website-and-app/). If you build on a composable service such as commercetools, add its subscription fee on top.

### Three-Year Cost Comparison

These are directional ranges from the sources above. Your quotes will vary with GMV, SKU count, and integrations.

| Cost line (3 years) | Shopify Plus | Adobe Commerce | Custom Commerce |
|---|---|---|---|
| Platform license | $82,800 base ($2,300 × 36) plus variable fee | $66,000–$570,000+ ($22K–$190K × 3) | $0, plus any composable SaaS fees |
| Implementation or migration | $25,000–$300,000+ | $100,000–$250,000 (mid-market B2B) | $25,000–$150,000 (mid-market) to $1M+ (enterprise) |
| Hosting | Included | Included on Cloud; separate on-premise | Your cloud bill |
| Upgrades and maintenance | Low: Shopify ships updates | High: patches and release-line upgrades | Highest: you own all of it |
| Why it matters | Lowest predictable run cost | Cost scales with customization and GMV | Pays off only if the custom logic earns revenue |

Cost changes again once B2B enters the picture, because B2B is where platform limits show up first.

## Which Is the Best Ecommerce Platform for B2B?

The best ecommerce platform for B2B depends on workflow complexity. Shopify Plus handles catalog pricing, payment terms, and deposits well. Adobe Commerce handles quotes, approvals, and requisition lists natively. Custom commerce fits B2B models that neither platform supports out of the box.

Shopify [opened native B2B to its Basic, Grow, and Advanced plans on April 2, 2026](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/04/03/shopify-adds-b2b-features-for-dtc-merchants/). Plus keeps the advanced tier: unlimited catalogs (non-Plus plans are [capped at 3](https://scandiweb.com/blog/shopify-b2b/)), deposits, and partial payments.

Here is how B2B capabilities compare:

| B2B capability | Shopify Plus | Adobe Commerce | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company accounts | Companies with locations; settings per location | Company accounts with hierarchy, roles, and permissions | Multi-branch buyers need separate addresses, terms, and users |
| Customer-specific pricing | Unlimited catalogs; lowest price wins when catalogs overlap | Shared catalogs and tier pricing | Contract pricing errors erode margin and trust |
| Payment terms | Net 7–90, due on fulfillment, deposits; [ACH for US buyers](https://www.shopify.com/enterprise/blog/shopify-winter-26-edition-b2b-roundup) | Payment on account with credit limits | Terms decide whether buyers can order online at all |
| Quotes and negotiation | Draft orders or apps | Native negotiable quotes | High-value deals rarely close at list price |
| Approval workflows | Apps or Shopify Functions | Native purchase-order approval rules | Procurement teams won't adopt a portal that skips approvals |
| Reordering | Quick order lists in B2B themes | Native quick order and requisition lists | Repeat orders are most of B2B volume |
| ERP integration | Prebuilt connectors and APIs | Extensions and APIs | ERP sync is the top cause of B2B project overruns |

Most B2B requirement lists are mostly standard. The few items that aren't, such as quotes, approvals, and contract pricing logic, decide the platform.

## Why Do Enterprise Ecommerce Replatforming Projects Fail?

Enterprise ecommerce replatforming projects usually fail for one unglamorous reason: teams plan for the platform they're moving to instead of the system they're leaving. The old store's ERP links, custom checkout logic, and data quality decide the budget. Projects tend to break in five places.

### 1. Budgeting for the Destination, Not the Origin

Teams collect platform quotes and call it a budget. But [replatforming cost tracks the complexity of the origin environment](https://elogic.co/blog/replatforming-cost-index/): mid-market B2B migrations that arrive with entry-level budgets usually turn out to be one or two tiers more complex once ERP and B2B requirements are listed. That isn't a migration estimate. It's a license quote.

### 2. Treating Checkout Logic as a Theme Task

Custom discounts, shipping rules, and payment logic live deep in the checkout. On Shopify, Scripts [could no longer be edited from April 15, 2026 and stopped running on June 30, 2026](https://shopify.dev/changelog/shopify-scripts-will-be-deprecated-on-june-30-2026); that logic must now run as Shopify Functions. Moving off Adobe Commerce means rebuilding custom checkout modules the same way. When this step is missed, discounts can fail silently and orders complete at full price.

### 3. Ignoring Version Debt

Adobe Commerce release lines have fixed support windows. [Regular support ended for 2.4.4 on April 14, 2026 and for 2.4.6 on August 11, 2026](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/commerce-operations/release/versions); extended support for 2.4.6 runs to August 31, 2027. Upgrading or migrating isn't the hard part. Doing it under a security deadline is.

### 4. Building Custom When the Differentiator Is Content

A headless front end [multiplies build cost by 1.5–2.5× and adds 8–16 weeks](https://elogic.co/blog/replatforming-cost-index/). If what sets you apart is brand storytelling rather than transaction logic, a themed or headless SaaS build gets you there for far less.

### 5. Integrations Without an Owner

ERP integration is the [number-one cost overrun driver](https://elogic.co/blog/replatforming-cost-index/) in replatforming, and the same benchmark recommends a 25%–40% contingency on any migration quote. If no one owns the integration layer, it becomes everyone's blocker. So, name the integration owner before you name the platform.

## When Should You Choose Shopify Plus, Adobe Commerce, or Custom Commerce?

Choose Shopify Plus when speed to market and low operating overhead matter most. Choose Adobe Commerce when you need deep customization and native complex B2B on one codebase. Choose custom commerce only when your transaction model can't run on either platform.

Here is a decision framework you can use with your team:

| Choose | When | Best for | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shopify Plus | Your requirements are mostly standard and you want to shrink your in-house platform team | DTC brands, mid-market B2B, multi-brand portfolios | Checkout limits; variable fee as GMV grows; app sprawl |
| Adobe Commerce | You have complex catalogs, quote-driven B2B, or deep ERP-specific logic | Manufacturers, distributors, multi-store enterprises | Upgrade cadence; specialist talent costs; GMV-based renewals |
| Custom / composable | Your pricing, fulfillment, or marketplace model is the product | Marketplaces, subscription hybrids, regulated commerce | Highest TCO; a permanent engineering team |
| Stay on Magento Open Source and upgrade | The platform fits, your team is strong, and you need time | Stable catalogs with a capable in-house team | Security patching; compressed upgrade windows |

In a Shopify Plus vs Magento decision, the real question is whether you want to keep owning hosting, patching, and upgrades. If you're on Magento Open Source and comparing Magento alternatives, the realistic enterprise shortlist is Shopify Plus, Adobe Commerce as a Cloud Service, [BigCommerce](https://www.aceinfoway.com/bigcommerce), Salesforce Commerce Cloud, and composable stacks on commercetools.

Let your requirements inventory decide, not the most polished demo.

## How Do You Migrate from Magento to Shopify Plus?

A migration from Magento to Shopify Plus takes 6–12 weeks for a simple store and 4–14 months for mid-market and enterprise B2B. It costs roughly $25,000 to $300,000+, depending on SKU count, ERP integration, and custom logic.

Here is what a Magento to Shopify Plus migration typically costs:

| Store profile | Estimated cost | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Simple: under 1,000 SKUs, B2C | $25,000–$50,000 | 6–12 weeks |
| Mid-market: one ERP, light B2B | $50,000–$120,000 | 4–8 months |
| Complex B2B or headless | $120,000–$300,000+ | 8–14 months |

*Sources: [Elogic Replatforming Cost Index 2026](https://elogic.co/blog/replatforming-cost-index/) (Magento-to-Shopify Plus cost bands; timelines from its general replatforming tiers).*

Here is the ecommerce platform migration process, step by step:

### 1. Audit the Origin Platform

List every extension, custom module, integration, and data source. Score data quality and map every live URL. This discovery phase usually takes up to 4 weeks, and skipping it is the most expensive saving you can make.

### 2. Map Every Custom Feature to Its New Home

Here is where common Magento features land on Shopify Plus:

| Magento / Adobe Commerce | Shopify Plus equivalent |
|---|---|
| Custom modules | Apps, custom apps, or Shopify Functions |
| Checkout customizations | Checkout UI extensions and Functions |
| Customer groups and tier pricing | B2B companies and catalogs |
| Multi-store views | Expansion stores or Shopify Markets |
| CMS pages and blocks | Theme sections and metaobjects |

### 3. Migrate and Clean the Data

Move products, customers, and order history, and clean them on the way. Customer passwords can't be carried over from Magento, so plan an account-activation email campaign before launch.

### 4. Protect Your SEO

Shopify uses fixed URL paths (`/products/`, `/collections/`), so almost every Magento URL changes. Build a 301 redirect for each one, and benchmark rankings and traffic before cutover.

### 5. Rebuild Integrations and Test

Reconnect the ERP, PIM, OMS, and 3PL systems. Prebuilt ERP connectors cost [roughly $5,000–$30,000](https://elogic.co/blog/replatforming-cost-index/); custom connectors cost more. Run user acceptance testing with real B2B accounts and load-test your peak traffic.

### 6. Cut Over and Stabilize

Freeze catalog changes, run a final delta migration, switch DNS, and staff a 30-day hypercare period for order, payment, and integration issues.

Migrating isn't just a data move. It's the one moment you can remove a decade of workarounds.

## The Future of Enterprise Ecommerce Platforms

The future of enterprise ecommerce platforms is SaaS-first, B2B-ready, and headless by default. Adobe now sells a SaaS edition, Shopify ships native B2B on every paid plan, and front ends increasingly change independently of the commerce back end. Most of it is already shipping. Three shifts stand out.

### Adobe Commerce Goes SaaS-Optional

With [Adobe Commerce as a Cloud Service and Adobe Commerce Optimizer generally available since June 2025](https://business.adobe.com/blog/faster-growth-with-the-new-adobe-commerce-optimizer), Adobe customers can move to SaaS without leaving Adobe. So Shopify Plus vs Adobe Commerce is no longer a straight SaaS-versus-self-managed choice. It's a choice between ecosystems.

### B2B Becomes Table Stakes

Shopify's B2B GMV grew [96% in 2025](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/02/12/shopify-revenue-b2b-sales-ai-2025/), and native B2B now reaches every paid Shopify plan. Basic B2B features no longer set platforms apart. Scale features do: catalog limits, approval logic, and ERP depth.

### The Front End Decouples from the Platform

Adobe's Commerce Optimizer adds a headless storefront on top of an existing back end, Shopify offers Hydrogen for headless builds, and composable vendors like commercetools keep their Leader status. More often, the platform becomes a back-end decision, and the front end can change without a full replatform.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Shopify Plus cheaper than Adobe Commerce?**
Usually, yes, on total cost. Shopify Plus starts at $2,300/month with hosting and updates included. Adobe Commerce licenses are estimated at $22,000–$190,000+ per year, before hosting, upgrades, and specialist developers. Adobe can still be cheaper for heavily customized B2B stores that would otherwise need many paid Shopify apps.

**How long does it take to migrate from Magento to Shopify Plus?**
Between 6 weeks and 14 months, depending on complexity. A simple B2C store under 1,000 SKUs can move in 6–12 weeks. A mid-market store with one ERP takes 4–8 months, and enterprise B2B with SAP or Oracle often takes 8–14 months, including integration testing and SEO redirects.

**What are the best Magento alternatives for enterprise brands?**
Shopify Plus, Adobe Commerce as a Cloud Service, BigCommerce, Salesforce Commerce Cloud, and composable stacks on commercetools. The right one depends on B2B complexity, customization needs, and in-house engineering capacity. Brands leaving Magento for lower overhead usually shortlist Shopify Plus and BigCommerce first.

**Is Adobe Commerce 2.4.6 still supported?**
Only with extended support. Regular support for the 2.4.6 release line ended on August 11, 2026, and extended support runs until August 31, 2027. Release lines 2.4.7 and 2.4.8 have regular support until May 31, 2028, and 2.4.9, released May 12, 2026, is supported until May 31, 2029.

**Can you build a headless store on Shopify Plus or Adobe Commerce?**
Yes, on both. Shopify Plus supports headless builds through the Storefront API and its Hydrogen framework. Adobe Commerce supports headless through GraphQL APIs and Adobe Commerce Optimizer. Expect headless to add 1.5–2.5× to build cost and 8–16 weeks to the timeline, so reserve it for clear performance or content needs.
