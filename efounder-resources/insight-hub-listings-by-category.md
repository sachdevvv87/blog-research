# eFounder Insight Hub: Holiday Guides by Store Size

Two holiday guides, each in three versions, one for each Insight Hub category (monthly revenue). Every version shares the same steps or replies. What changes is the cover story, who it is for, where to start, the thumbnail and the listing copy.

| Insight Hub category | Holiday Store Speed Guide | Holiday Customer Service Replies |
|---|---|---|
| Up to $1,000 a month | eFounder-Holiday-Store-Speed-Guide-Up-to-1K.pdf | eFounder-Holiday-Customer-Service-Replies-Up-to-1K.pdf |
| $1,001 to $5,000 a month | eFounder-Holiday-Store-Speed-Guide-1K-to-5K.pdf | eFounder-Holiday-Customer-Service-Replies-1K-to-5K.pdf |
| Over $5,000 a month | eFounder-Holiday-Store-Speed-Guide-Over-5K.pdf | eFounder-Holiday-Customer-Service-Replies-Over-5K.pdf |

Every PDF is 4 pages. Every thumbnail is 1200 x 630 px. Form fields for every version: Name, Email ID. Form button for every version: **Send Me the Guide**.
