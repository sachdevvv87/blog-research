## 1. Peak Traffic Speed Checklist

**Title**
Will Your Store Keep Up With the Holiday Rush?

**Short description** (137 characters: for the resource card, meta description or social post)
A free, plain-English checklist for Shopify store owners: 17 simple steps to keep your store fast from Black Friday to the January sales.

**Long description** (for the resource page)
The holiday season brings more shoppers to your store than any other time of year, and they arrive in rushes: Black Friday, the last days before Christmas, Boxing Day and the January sales. If your store is slow on those days, shoppers don't wait. They leave and buy somewhere else.

This free checklist walks you through 17 simple steps, in the order you'd do them, with exactly where to click in Shopify. No coding and no developer needed. Do the five Level 1 steps in about an hour, or all 17 in about three.

## 2. Holiday Customer Service Replies

**Title**
What If You Could Answer Holiday Customer Messages in Seconds?

**Short description** (130 characters: for the resource card, meta description or social post)
15 ready-to-send replies to the questions Shopify customers ask most over the holidays, from "Where is my order?" to gift returns.

**Long description** (for the resource page)
From Black Friday to January returns, the same few questions fill your inbox again and again. "Where is my order?" "Will it arrive before Christmas?" "My code didn't work." Typing each answer from scratch takes hours, and tired answers can give the wrong date.

This free guide gives you 15 friendly, ready-written replies, sorted by where your customer is: before they buy, while they wait, when something goes wrong and after the holidays. It also shows you how to save them in Gmail or Shopify Inbox. Copy, fill in the blanks, send.
