# eFounder Insight Hub: Listing Details and Email Template Values

Two holiday resources for Shopify store owners. The same content is listed in all three Insight Hub categories (monthly revenue: up to $1,000, $1,001 to $5,000, and over $5,000), with a different problem statement for each category.

Written to the COO's founding principles: real value for a Shopify merchant, short (4 pages each), easy for anyone to follow, and no QeRetail language (no audit, checklist, scorecard or "fix problems").

# Change Needed in the Email Template

The email template still uses QeRetail-style words that readers see. Under the COO's principles, these should change before either email goes out:

| Current text | Change to |
|---|---|
| Heading: "Your Checklist is Ready" | Your Free Guide is Ready |
| "Download your checklist and start your first audit today." | Download it now and start today. |
| Button: "Download the Checklist" | Download Now |
| Form button on the old listing: "Send Me the Checklist" | Send Me the Guide |

The merge tag names ({{checklist_title}}, {{checklist_intro}}) are never shown to readers, so they can stay. Renaming them to {{resource_title}} and {{resource_intro}} would keep things tidy for future resources.

# Before Publishing: COO Check

| Question | Holiday Store Speed Guide | Holiday Customer Service Replies |
|---|---|---|
| Real value for a Shopify merchant? | Yes. Eight steps a store owner can do in Shopify admin in under 2 hours, before the next holiday sale. | Yes. Eight replies to the questions holiday customers actually send, ready to paste into Gmail or Shopify Inbox. |
| Short, not a dump of material? | 4 pages. Cut from 17 steps to 8. | 4 pages. Cut from 15 replies to 8. |
| Easy for anyone to follow? | Plain words, one "Why" and one "How" per step, and the exact place to click. | Short replies, with blanks in [brackets] and four simple rules. |
| Free of QeRetail language? | Yes. No audit, checklist, scorecard, score or "fix". | Yes. |
